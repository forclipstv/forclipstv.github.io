1. Who we are

ForClips (private, non-commercial project). Contact: forclipstv@gmail.com.

2. What the ClipFarm app does

ClipFarm is a desktop tool used only by the operator of ForClips. It connects to the operator's own YouTube and TikTok accounts through the official APIs (Google OAuth, TikTok Login Kit) to upload short video clips to those accounts and to read the performance statistics of those uploads. It has no other users.

ClipFarm uses YouTube API Services. By using ClipFarm with a YouTube account you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. The Google Privacy Policy applies to data processed by Google.

3. Data accessed through Google / YouTube and TikTok

  • YouTube (scopes youtube.upload, youtube.readonly, yt-analytics.readonly): upload videos to the connected channel, read the channel name to confirm which channel is connected, and read view, like and comment counts and YouTube Analytics metrics (average view duration, average percentage viewed) of the videos uploaded by ClipFarm.
  • TikTok (scopes user.info.basic, video.publish, video.upload, video.list): read the account's display name and privacy options, publish videos or send them as drafts to the connected account, and read view, like, comment and share counts of the account's public videos.

We do not access, collect or store data of other users, viewers, followers, comments or messages.

4. How data is used, stored and shared

Access and refresh tokens and the collected statistics (dated snapshots of the counts listed above, per uploaded video) are stored only locally on the operator's computer, protected by the operating system's access controls, and are used only to upload clips and to evaluate which clips perform well. Data is transferred only via encrypted HTTPS connections to the official APIs. It is never shared, sold or transferred to third parties and is not shown to anyone but the operator. ClipFarm shows no advertising and sets no cookies. At least every 30 days ClipFarm checks that its authorization is still valid and that the videos still exist; statistics of deleted videos and of revoked accounts are deleted. Data obtained through Google APIs is handled in accordance with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Revoking access and deletion

Access can be revoked at any time via the Google security settings at security.google.com/settings/security/permissions (Google and YouTube) or in the TikTok app under Settings, Security, Apps and services. After a revocation, the related stored data is deleted within 7 calendar days. You can also request deletion of all data stored by ClipFarm at any time by writing to forclipstv@gmail.com; it is deleted as soon as possible and within 7 calendar days. Deleting data stored by ClipFarm does not delete videos or data stored by YouTube or TikTok themselves.

6. This website

This website sets no cookies and uses no tracking or analytics. The hosting provider may process technical access data such as the IP address to deliver the page.

7. Your rights (GDPR)

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Contact: forclipstv@gmail.com.
