HomeTerms of Service

1. Who we are

ForClips (private, non-commercial project). Contact: forclipstv@gmail.com.

2. What the ClipFarm app does

ClipFarm is a desktop tool used only by the operator of ForClips. It connects to the operator's own YouTube and TikTok accounts through the official APIs (Google OAuth, TikTok Login Kit) to upload short video clips to those accounts. It has no other users.

3. Data accessed through Google / YouTube and TikTok

  • YouTube (scopes youtube.upload, youtube.readonly): upload videos to the connected channel and read the channel name to confirm which channel is connected.
  • TikTok (scopes user.info.basic, video.publish, video.upload): read the account's display name and privacy options, and publish videos to the connected account.

We do not access, collect or store data of other users, viewers, followers, comments or messages.

4. Storage and security

Access and refresh tokens are stored only locally on the operator's computer and are never shared, sold or transferred to third parties. Data obtained through Google APIs is used only for the upload function described above and is handled in accordance with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Revoking access and deletion

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions (Google and YouTube) or in the TikTok app under Settings, Security, Apps and services. Locally stored tokens are deleted on request or when an account is disconnected. Requests: forclipstv@gmail.com.

6. This website

This website sets no cookies and uses no tracking or analytics. The hosting provider may process technical access data such as the IP address to deliver the page.

7. Your rights (GDPR)

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Contact: forclipstv@gmail.com.
